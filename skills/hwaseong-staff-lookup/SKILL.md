# Hwaseong Staff Lookup

1. Determine location, jurisdiction, issue type and administrative stage before searching by keyword.
2. Distinguish city hall, district office, eup/myeon/dong, business office and external-agency responsibility.
3. Treat staff-search results as evidence, not as a simple keyword match.
4. Do not put case numbers, complainant details, precise private addresses or other sensitive case facts into public search terms.
5. If assignment is unclear, report the likely routing and the remaining verification instead of guessing.

This is an organization-specific example. A recipient should replace the search URL, organization levels, phone conventions and routing rules if their environment differs.
